HeadFocus
=========
Look at a monitor and your mouse jumps there, ready to type.

START
  Start Menu > HeadFocus
  The first time, it calibrates: for each monitor the mouse jumps to its
  center. Press Enter, then look at the mouse for about 5 seconds.

KEYS
  F9       Pause / resume (works from any app)
  Ctrl+C   Quit (in the HeadFocus window)

RECALIBRATE (moved the camera, your chair, or monitors)
  Start Menu > HeadFocus - Calibrate

SEE WHAT THE CAMERA SEES
  Start Menu > HeadFocus - Preview (camera window)

UNINSTALL
  Settings > Apps > Installed apps > HeadFocus > Uninstall
  or: Start Menu > Uninstall HeadFocus
  This removes the program, its shortcuts, and your calibration.

PRIVACY
  Runs 100% offline. Camera frames are analysed in memory and never saved
  or sent anywhere. The only stored data is your calibration (a few numbers)
  in %APPDATA%\HeadFocus\config.json.
